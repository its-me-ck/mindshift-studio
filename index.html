Mindshift Studio

Psychology, explained with stick figures. Mindshift Studio is a YouTube channel that breaks down research on focus, habits and how the mind works into short animated explainers.

Watch on YouTube

Mindshift Uploader

Mindshift Uploader is a private tool the channel owner uses to upload Mindshift Studio videos, thumbnails and captions to YouTube through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public, and only the channel owner signs in to it.
